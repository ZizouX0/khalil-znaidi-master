# 3. The programmes

*All figures checked 17 August 2026 unless marked otherwise. Tuition is the non-EU rate.*

**How to read the tiers:**

- **Tier A — you could apply now.** Taught in English or French. No new language needed.
- **Tier B — one extra step.** Needs Spanish or Italian, which you could reach in roughly 6–12 months as a French speaker.
- **Tier C — a longer commitment.** German, Czech, Polish or a Nordic language.

Everything listed is a **genuine master's degree**. Programmes that turned out to be certificates, *títulos propios*, Italian *Master di I livello* or school diplomas have been excluded and are named at the end so nobody re-finds them.

## Direction 3 — Music marketing and artist promotion

**Be prepared for this: as a standalone, affordable, state-recognised master's, this direction essentially does not exist in Europe.**

That is a finding, not a search failure. Six independent researchers looked — across France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Poland, Czechia, the whole Erasmus Mundus catalogue, and the applied-sciences sector in eleven countries. Results:

- **Spain and Portugal:** zero
- **Italy:** zero *laurea magistrale*
- **Benelux:** zero music-specific degrees anywhere
- **Erasmus Mundus:** zero, in all 220 funded programmes. The word "marketing" appears in **none** of the 220 titles
- **Applied sciences, eleven countries:** zero

**What actually exists:**

| Programme | Where | Cost/yr | Reality check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lyon 2 — M1 Management de l'innovation → M2 Management Carrières d'Artistes / Écosystème de la Musique** | France, public | ≈€4,055 | **The best route in this direction.** Admits at **M1** from "L3 économie et/ou gestion" with no credit numeral — so your 146 is fine — then progresses internally to the music M2. This solves France's M2-only trap |
| U. Silesia — Creative Management in New Media | Poland | €3,080 | **Hard fail.** Requires an arts/media bachelor **plus a portfolio review.** You have neither |

Music marketing exists in Europe as a *module inside* Direction 4 programmes, or as a *sector you enter* from a Direction 1 or 2 degree. It is not a degree you can go and buy.

## Excluded — and why

So you don't waste time rediscovering these:

| What | Why excluded |
|---|---|
| **Every Italian "music business master"** — LUISS BS, Sapienza MMCM, Roma Tre, IULM, 24ORE | All *Master di I livello*: 1 year, 60–65 CFU. **Not** a second-cycle degree |
| **Nearly every Spanish "máster en industria musical"** — UC3M (€5,900), IL3-UB, UAM, SAE | *Formación permanente* / *título propio* — not state master's. Likely fails the EQF-6 requirement for Spain's post-study permit |
| **Berklee Valencia** | ≈€48,000 |
| **UK music-business master's** | £16,000–31,900. Only Westminster survives, as a stretch |
| **Popakademie, BIMM, Catalyst Berlin, SAE, ICMP, Saint Louis Rome** | Fail on accreditation, price, or both |
| **All AI/data Erasmus Mundus programmes** | Require a CS/engineering/maths degree |
| **EMMIE (Erasmus Mundus)** | Requires **240 ECTS** — unrecoverable |
| **Queen's Belfast (£23,000), Ulster (£18,310)** | Northern Ireland is not the cheap UK route it appears to be |
| **SGH music-management course** | A *podyplomowe* diploma, not a master's |
| **Any French *diplôme d'établissement* / DU** | Not a national degree |
| **Every French *Mastère Spécialisé*, without exception** | CGE's own rules require **Bac+5** (or Bac+4 plus 3 years' experience). You hold Bac+3, so all are structurally out of reach. The MS is a trademark, not a *diplôme national*, and does not confer the *grade de master*. **Warning:** ESART advertises a "Mastère Spécialisé" without CGE membership, on an RNCP registration **expiring 31 Aug 2026** |
| **Rome Business School, EU Business School, BSBI Berlin, IMB Barcelona** | Accreditation. Rome BS awards a Spanish *título propio*, not an Italian degree; **EU Business School is rated H− by Germany's anabin**; BSBI awards no German degree |
| **ESSCA** | Requires a 4-year bachelor *or* 3 years' experience |
| **ISEG** | Its RNCP titles are valid, but no *grade*, no *visa*, not CGE, no AACSB/EQUIS/AMBA, ~€10,150/yr, and its bac+5 tracks are **alternance-only** — unobtainable from abroad |
| **Uniarts Helsinki MA Music Business** | Perfect subject fit, €28,000/yr, verified **zero** scholarships |

## What is missing from this list — read this

**Since the first draft, all the interrupted research streams have been completed and a verification pass has been run** over the shortlist — it corrected three material figures. What remains genuinely open:

1. **Ten of France's ~30 IAEs were never reached** — Metz, Amiens, Orléans, Limoges, Perpignan, Pau, Savoie, Gustave Eiffel, Brest, Valenciennes. The tool to finish the job is `iae-france.fr/formations/` with its *format* filter. IAE Bordeaux returned a server error and is unverified.
2. **Austria:** FH St. Pölten (site blocked), FH Vorarlberg, and several public universities are unverified.
3. **Nordics:** Inland Norway (INN) and USN look like the best remaining leads at roughly NOK 58,200; Aalborg's non-EU deadline is unconfirmed, as is the final Norwegian parliamentary vote on repealing fees.
4. **Private schools:** NEOMA, Audencia, KEDGE, TBS and Montpellier BS have aggregator figures only; EADA and Vlerick were not reached.
5. **No music-business sweep in Polish or Hungarian**; Portugal's Universidade Católica pages were down; CIMEA's fees and turnaround remain unverified.

**The most important caveat of all: almost every 2027/28 deadline in these documents is unpublished.** GIOCA's listed rounds are March/April 2026 and are **stale**. FH Kufstein's 3 March is confirmed for 2026/27 and has passed. Stipendium Hungaricum's 2027/28 call is not open. **Treat every date here as an indication of *when in the year* to look, not as a deadline to diarise.**
