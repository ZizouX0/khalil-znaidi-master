## 1. Bottom line

The IAE network is now **enumerated, not sampled**. I scraped the full
`iae-france.fr/formations/` catalogue — **106 pages, 921 unique programmes,
37 network schools** — and parsed IAE, level, domain, city, admission conditions
and *format* for every one, then verified the marketing hits on the institutions'
own sites. The result: **eight new M1-entry, formation-initiale programmes**, one
of which (**IAE Gustave Eiffel / Paris-Est, Master MMX**) is the strongest CRM fit
found anywhere in France so far — M1 entry from a licence, formation initiale
genuinely available, non-EU applicants routed explicitly through Études en France,
and an M2 curriculum containing *CRM et Data Marketing* and *IA et digital
Marketing*. Two of the ten unreached IAEs (**Limoges, Pau-Bayonne**) have no
marketing master at all; two more (**Orléans, Brest**) have one but only in
alternance; **Perpignan** is M2-only. **IAE Bordeaux is resolved** — the 503 that
blocked 2A2 was transient, and it is an M1-entry formation-initiale master with
apprenticeship confined to M2. Separately I **closed 2A2's open ACTION on
ISM-IAE Versailles MRC**: it is *exclusively* alternance, not an exception.

**The most important structural finding: the catalogue's `format` facet
over-reports "Initiale".** Four programmes tagged *Initiale, Alternance, Continue*
turn out, once the fiche prose or the university page is read, to be
alternance-only or alternance-in-practice. The facet is a *discovery* tool, never
a *screening* one.

## 6. Structural findings to add to 2A and 2A2

1. **`iae-france.fr`'s `format` facet is unreliable in one direction only.** It
   never hides an alternance option, but it **over-reports "Initiale"**. Confirmed
   false or misleading on Valenciennes MDC, Versailles MRC, Montpellier CSRC and
   Savoie *Stratégie digitale* — all tagged *Initiale* yet alternance-bound in the
   fiche prose or on the university site. **Screening rule: facet → fiche *Profil*
   and *Professionnalisation* blocks → institution page. Three steps, never one.**
2. **The Score IAE-Message is near-universal but not universal, and the exceptions
   are worth a lot to a 12–13/20 file.** From the full census: **IAE Metz requires
   no SIM** (dossier + entretien only) — joining 2A's IAE Montpellier as the second
   confirmed SIM-free door. SIM is mandatory at Bordeaux, Saint-Étienne, Amiens,
   Rouen, Bretagne Sud, Gustave Eiffel/Paris-Est.
3. **Apprenticeship scoping language is the tell.** Compare Gustave Eiffel's
   *« l'admission définitive **en alternance** est conditionnée… »* (scoped to one
   route — safe) against Bretagne Sud's *« admission définitive conditionnée à la
   signature d'un contrat d'alternance »* (unscoped — fatal). **One preposition
   separates a usable programme from an unusable one.** Read for the scope word.
4. **Apprenticeship confined to M2 is the second safe pattern** (IAE Bordeaux). He
   enters M1 in formation initiale and, once he holds a French residence permit
   and a year of local network, the M2 apprenticeship becomes genuinely available —
   turning the trap into a funded second year. **This is the best structure in the
   whole French sweep and should be weighted accordingly.**
5. **Small IAEs are not a hidden reserve.** Five of the ten unreached institutes
   (Limoges, Pau, Orléans, Brest, Perpignan) yielded nothing usable. The French
   marketing-master pool is concentrated in the mid-size and large IAEs, which the
   first two passes had already reached. **The network is now closed; further
   French search effort belongs outside the IAEs (Task 3), not inside them.**
