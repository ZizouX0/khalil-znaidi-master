## 1. Bottom line

**Belgium (Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles) is the single strongest country in this
scope and probably one of the strongest in the whole project for this student.**
Everything is taught in his native language, the total non-EU cost is a *verified*
**€5,369/year** (€1,194 minerval + €4,175 supplementary contribution, Décret-programme
of 11 December 2024), he is **explicitly exempt from any French test** at ULiège
because his prior education was in French, and Belgium grants a **12-month job-search
permit with unrestricted labour-market access**. Crucially, I have now **verified on
primary sources** the thing Phase 1 listed as unverified: **French-speaking Belgium
does have a formal bridging mechanism** — ULB's jury may impose *"un programme
complémentaire de maximum 60 crédits"*, UCLouvain Saint-Louis the same, and IHECS
spells it out in decree terms (>15 extra credits = an *année préparatoire non
diplômante*). So the 146-ECTS problem in Belgium is **survivable even in the worst
case**, at the cost of one extra bridging year. The two hard cautions are (a) the
180-credit wording really is literal in FWB access rules, and (b) **ULiège imposes a
published minimum average of 12/20** — he clears it by nothing at all. Luxembourg is
thin: the University of Luxembourg has **no marketing and no music master's**, only a
tangential Entrepreneurship & Innovation route, though tuition is ~€400/year and
non-EU pay the same. The **Netherlands survives only on the *Licence Appliquée*
branch**, where AUAS's Master Digital Driven Business (English, 1 year, with a
Marketing specialisation) is the best *content* fit I found anywhere in Benelux — but
it costs €10,648 and dies instantly on the *Fondamentale* branch. Directions 3 and 4
(music) yield **nothing music-specific in any of the three countries**; the honest
best available are cultural/creative-industries management degrees.

## 3. Country-level facts (verified)

### 🇧🇪 Belgium

**Non-EU tuition, Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles — VERIFIED (two independent primary sources)**

| Component | Amount 2026-27 |
|---|---|
| Minerval, default rate ("sans condition particulière") | **€1,194** |
| (intermediate rate, if a means condition is granted) | €835 |
| **Contribution supplémentaire, non-EU** (Décret-programme 11 Dec 2024) | **€4,175** |
| **TOTAL for a Tunisian student** | **€5,369 / year** |

Exemptions from the €4,175 are limited to: **UN Least-Developed-Country nationals**
(Tunisia is **not** an LDC → **he pays**), holders of a CESS from the French Community
after two years' schooling there, AESS/teaching masters, WBI scholarship holders, and
"assimilated" students. Transitional rules preserve the older *Droit d'Inscription
Spécifique* (DIS = €1,487 for bachelor) only for students already enrolled in 2024-25 —
**not applicable to a 2027 entrant**.
Sources: [ICHEC official fee page 2026-27](https://www.ichec.be/fr/actualites/frais-detudes-les-montants-prevus-pour-2026-2027) · [ULiège droits d'inscription](https://www.enseignement.uliege.be/cms/c_17654509/fr/droits-d-inscription) — both checked 2026-08-17.

**French test exemption — VERIFIED at ULiège, and it is generous.** ULiège requires B2
in the language of instruction *unless exempt*, and exempts: applicants **whose native
language is French**, applicants **holding the nationality of a French-speaking
country**, and applicants who **completed secondary or higher education in French**.
This student qualifies on at least the first and third grounds (FSEG Sousse teaches
management in French). **No DELF/DALF/TCF needed.** (C1 is required only for *masters
en enseignement*, which is irrelevant here.)
Source: [ULiège — Conditions d'accès Master hors UE](https://www.enseignement.uliege.be/cms/c_9096818/fr/conditions-d-acces-master-hors-ue), checked 2026-08-17.
⚠️ studyinbelgium.be's generic page does **not** state the exemption — it only lists
B2 + DALF/DELF/TEF/TCF. So the exemption must be confirmed **per institution**; it is
verified for ULiège, **unverified for UCLouvain, ULB, ICHEC and IHECS**.

**Bridging mechanism — VERIFIED (this closes a Phase-1 gap).**
- **ULB** (Master en sciences de gestion): access for foreign degree-holders if the jury
  values the prior studies at **at least 180 credits**; *"Le jury peut imposer un
  programme complémentaire de maximum 60 crédits."* (via search extract of
  `www2.ulb.ac.be/temp/programme/MA-GEST/conditions-acces.html` — page 403s on direct fetch)
- **UCLouvain Saint-Louis Bruxelles**: admission *sur dossier* — outcome is **direct
  access, complementary training of max 60 ECTS, or refusal**; up to 45 of those credits
  drawn from Saint-Louis bachelor programmes.
- **IHECS** (haute école, decree wording): *"L'étudiant peut être amené à suivre des
  enseignements complémentaires pour un maximum de 60 crédits supplémentaires. Lorsque
  la charge supplémentaire dépasse 15 crédits, elle constitue une année d'études
  préparatoire, non diplômante et est considérée comme la dernière année d'un premier
  cycle qui donne accès aux études visées."*
  Source: [IHECS — Inscriptions/passerelles](https://www.ihecs.be/fr/master-en-publicite/inscriptions), checked 2026-08-17.

**Implication:** a 34-credit shortfall, if a jury insists on counting it, is **inside**
the 60-credit Belgian cap — unlike Germany (30) or Austria (30). Belgium is the
continental country where the credit gap is *most* fixable. ⚠️ Note the IHECS wording:
above 15 credits it becomes a **non-degree preparatory year**, i.e. **+1 year and +1
year of tuition/living cost**, not a free bolt-on.

**Post-study work — VERIFIED.** Third-country graduates may extend their stay **up to
12 months** to find a job or start a business (*année de recherche d'emploi*). During
that year there is **unrestricted access to the labour market** — any employer, any
function, no work permit. The change-of-status application must be filed **at least 15
days before the current residence card expires**. The Office des Étrangers may run a
check **after 3 months** on the applicant's real prospects.
Source: [SPF Affaires étrangères — Année pour recherche d'un emploi après les études](https://brazil.diplomatie.belgium.be/fr/visa-pour-la-belgique/long-sejour-en-belgique-plus-de-90-jours-visa-d/travail-visa-d/annee-pour-recherche-dun-emploi-apres-les-etudes), checked 2026-08-17.

**Cost of living / visa financial proof.** Official non-EU student *minimum means of
subsistence* **2026-27 = €1,062/month**, i.e. an annual financial plan of **€12,744
plus registration fees** (studyinbelgium.be, checked 2026-08-17). ULiège separately
demands a financing plan showing **€10,000+** and charges a **non-refundable €200
application fee**. ⚠️ Real monthly student budgets by city (Brussels vs Liège vs
Mons/Namur — Brussels materially dearer) **NOT VERIFIED**; treat €1,062 as the legal
floor, not a realistic Brussels budget.

**Deadline pattern.** Both ULiège and UCLouvain apply **31 March** as the hard deadline
for applicants who are non-EU nationality **and** not resident in Belgium **and**
requiring a visa (ULiège 2026-27: applications open 18 Feb 2026, close 31 Mar 2026).
studyinbelgium.be advises enrolling "as soon as February and before 31 March."
**For autumn 2027 expect ~mid-Feb → 31 March 2027 — NOT YET PUBLISHED, treat as indicative.**

## 7. Recommended action list (Benelux-specific)

1. **Read the diploma.** *Fondamentale* or *Appliquée*. Decides the Netherlands entirely. Free.
2. **Get the FSEG attestation** (6 semesters / 3 years / **180 credits** / grants access to Mastère in Tunisia) + the *supplément au diplôme*. In FWB the 180 wording is literal; this document is the whole ballgame.
3. **Email admissions with the transcript before applying**, at: **ULiège** (the 12/20 floor and the 75 %-per-year rule are both live risks), **UCLouvain LSM**, **ULB Solvay**, **ICHEC** and **IHECS** (IHECS's non-EU degree-seeking route is undocumented online). Ask each one explicitly: *(a)* do you count the 146 or the 180? *(b)* would you impose *crédits complémentaires*, and how many? *(c)* am I exempt from a French test given French-medium higher education?
4. **Calendar:** Belgium's francophone universities close on **31 March**; **UGent closes 1 February**; **AUAS closes 1 April for non-EU**; **Luxembourg's non-EU window closes before February**. The earliest of these governs — start the file in **autumn 2026**.
5. **Budget €5,369 + ~€12,744 living** ≈ **€18,100 for a Belgian year**, plus a €200 ULiège application fee. This is the cheapest credible route in the whole Benelux.
6. **Take IELTS anyway.** It costs one test and unlocks Solvay, LSM's English track, UGent and AUAS. Without it he is confined to the French-taught programmes.
