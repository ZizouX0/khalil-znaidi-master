# Raw research

The 37 source reports behind the six deliverables in [`../output/`](../output).

**Why these are here.** The deliverables are about **9% of what the research produced** — 15,000 published words against 175,000 gathered. Everything that got compressed out lives here: **source URLs, check dates, the exact admission wording quoted from each university, per-city cost of living, per-programme deadlines, and each agent's own statement of what it searched and found nothing on.**

If you want to check a figure, extend the search, or understand *why* a conclusion was reached, start here rather than with the deliverables.

## How the research was structured

A phased plan. Phase 1 was a hard gate — nothing else ran until the credential question was answered, because its result defined the search space.

| Phase | What ran |
|---|---|
| **1** | Credential eligibility — is the 146 real, and which countries are open? |
| **2** | One agent per country |
| **3** | Thematic sweeps cutting across countries |
| **4** | Independent verification of the shortlist |
| **5** | Consolidation into the deliverables |

## Phase 4 — verification, and the contradictions

These are the most useful files in the corpus, because they are where claims were **overturned**.

| File | What it corrected |
|---|---|
| **`PHASE4-verification.md`** | Independently re-checked the shortlist. **Three material corrections**, including retracting the Tunisian government grant this project had led with, and killing the idea that FH Kufstein's medium-of-instruction letter could replace an English test |
| **`CONTRADICTION-01-ireland-marketing-permits.md`** | One agent claimed Ireland bars marketing roles from work permits. The official list bars only estate agents and conference organisers — **the claim was wrong**, and would have wrongly removed Ireland |
| **`CONTRADICTION-02-rncp7-post-study-permit.md`** | One agent held French RNCP-7 schools off the shortlist over post-study rights. Tracing the statute showed niveau 7 **is** on the qualifying list. Also independently confirmed the 1.5×SMIC threshold another agent could not verify |

## Reading these critically

- **`NOT VERIFIED — check directly`** means exactly that: the agent could not confirm it on an official source and refused to estimate. Treat those as open questions.
- **Every agent states what it found nothing on.** Those coverage statements are how the gaps in this project were found — read them before assuming something doesn't exist.
- **Some reports contradict each other.** Where that happened it is recorded and resolved in the contradiction files above, not averaged away. Where a later pass superseded an earlier one — Finland, Norway, Germany — both are kept so the reasoning is visible.
- **Dates are mostly from the 2026/27 cycle.** Almost no 2027/28 deadline was published at the time of research.
