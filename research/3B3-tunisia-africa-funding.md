## 1. BOTTOM LINE

The Tunisian *prêt universitaire à l'étranger* **exists, is real, is open to master's students, and he is very
likely eligible** — but it is worth only **5,000 TND/year (~€1,450) for EU countries, 6,000 TND (~€1,750) for
France/Germany**, it is **competitive** (ranked within a fixed budget), it is **means-tested with a ceiling**,
and — the decisive structural point — **it is granted only to students already enrolled abroad**, applied for in
the **October–December window of the academic year itself**. It therefore cannot fund the visa financial-guarantee
file, cannot pay a first-year deposit, and cannot be counted on before he is already in Europe. It is a
**partial reimbursement of year 1 and a real contribution to year 2**, not a funding plan. Its terms are
nonetheless excellent: **repayment over up to 10 years, starting 2 years after the last disbursement**, and no
interest rate appears anywhere in the governing texts (`NOT VERIFIED`). **AUF is a negative result**: the AUF has
no master's mobility scholarship an individual Tunisian student can apply for — its only student-facing
instrument is a partial tuition subsidy for *distance* degrees. Tunisian **bank** study loans are all
savings-plan-linked (BIAT CREDINAJAH, STB Prêt Épargne Études, Amen Campus, BT Avenir) and require a plan opened
**3 years earlier**; without one, the only bank route is an ordinary *crédit personnel* in a salaried parent's
name. The one genuinely new competitive find is the **Billboard France × Amazon Music bourse** — full funding of
one master's year in a French institution, music-industry focus, financial need weighted, ~15 July deadline —
which maps directly onto directions 3 and 4. Almost every Africa/MENA scheme checked **excludes him**, and the
reasons are documented below. Separately, the **BCT exchange-control ceilings are NOT a constraint**: circulaire
10-2025 allows 6,000 TND installation + 4,000 TND/month living + tuition at invoice value.

## 3. THE TUNISIAN STUDENT LOAN — FULL ANSWER (Task 1)

### 3.1 — MESRS *Prêt universitaire à l'étranger* / القرض الجامعي بالخارج ⭐ **THE INSTRUMENT HE CAN ACTUALLY GET**

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Awarding body** | Ministère de l'Enseignement Supérieur et de la Recherche Scientifique — **Direction Générale des Affaires Estudiantines, Direction des Bourses et des Prêts**. Portal **www.best.rnu.tn**. |
| **Legal basis** | Décret n° 2009-3040 du 19 octobre 2009 (bourses nationales et prêts universitaires), amended by décret gouvernemental n° 2018-927 du 13 nov 2018 · arrêté du 26 octobre 2009 (conditions et modalités), amended 9 oct 2012 and 5 mars 2019 · **arrêté du 24 novembre 2010 fixing the amounts, amended by arrêté du 18 février 2019, JORT n° 16 du 22 février 2019, p. 538** (read directly). |
| **Does it exist for master's study abroad?** | **YES.** The scheme's stated scope is *"les étudiants tunisiens poursuivant leurs stages ou leurs études à l'étranger"* — no level restriction, no field restriction, no age limit anywhere in the published conditions. The parallel domestic loan explicitly tiers by level up to *Mastère année 2*, which corroborates that master's is in scope. |

**Eligibility — the exact published conditions**

1. **Nationality:** Tunisian. Registration on the BEST system is possible **only with the CIN (carte d'identité
   nationale)** — no other document is accepted.
2. **Enrolment status — THE CRITICAL ONE:** the call is addressed to *"الطلبة الدارسين بالخارج"* — **students
   already studying abroad**. This is the opposite of the ruled-out *bourse*, which required current enrolment
   in Tunisia. **He becomes eligible only once he is enrolled in Europe**, not before.
3. **Means test:** granted *"si le revenu net annuel déclaré des parents ne dépasse pas un montant fixé par
   arrêté"*. There **is** a ceiling. Its value is `NOT VERIFIED` (see gap 1). Note the widely-repeated Arabic
   gloss that loans go to families *above* the scholarship threshold is describing the **domestic** loan; for
   the abroad loan the ministry's own French text states a ceiling that must **not** be exceeded. Treat as: a
   band, not a floor.
4. **Competitive ranking within budget** (*"في حدود الاعتمادات المرصودة"*), on four published criteria:
   - النجاح والتميّز والتفوّق في المسار الجامعي — academic success and distinction **← his weakest point at 12–13/20**
   - الاختصاصات ذات الأولوية بالنسبة للبلاد التونسية — fields prioritised by Tunisia **← marketing is not a
     stated priority field; this is a soft weighting, not an exclusion**
   - المؤسسات الجامعية المتميّزة إقليميًا ودوليًا — the standing of the host institution
   - الوضعية الاجتماعية للطالب — the student's social situation **← this one helps him**
5. **Guarantor required:** the *tuteur* must sign a ***cautionnement solidaire*** (تعهّد بالتضامن) undertaking to
   repay the entire loan on the student's default. A parent must be willing and creditworthy.

**Amount — verified from the JORT table (arrêté 18 février 2019, art. 4 nouveau), annual, in TND**

| Country of study | Annual loan |
|---|---|
| **European countries** (البلدان الأوروبية) | **5,000 TND** |
| **France, Germany**, Canada, USA | **6,000 TND** |
| Japan, China, South Korea, India | 4,000 TND |
| Arab Maghreb Union countries | 3,000 TND |
| Other Asian countries incl. Russia, Turkey | 3,000 TND |
| African, South American countries, Australia | 2,500 TND |

At ~3.4 TND/EUR that is **≈ €1,450/yr for Italy, Spain, Poland, Hungary etc., ≈ €1,750/yr for France**.
Roughly **€120–145 per month**. Material but not transformative.

**Interest:** no rate is stated in any source located. `NOT VERIFIED — check directly.` (For contrast, the
**CNSS/CNRPS** university loan — a different instrument, §3.2 — explicitly bears **5%/yr**, so the state loans
are not automatically free.)

**Repayment:** duration **not exceeding 10 years**, beginning **two years after the date of the last university
loan received**. Signed as a contract, four copies, each bearing a 5 TND fiscal stamp, with the guarantor's
countersignature, returned within 30 days of the contract being made available on his BEST account.

**Disbursement:** by bank or postal transfer **in the student's name to a Tunisian account** (per the 2019
Finance Law rules on cash). Not paid abroad — he needs a Tunisian account and someone to move it.

**Deadline — 2027 cycle.** The pattern is fixed: call opens **~19–24 October**, the online form on
`best.rnu.tn` closes **~30 November**, and the **paper file must be physically deposited at the ministry's
bureau d'ordre central** (Direction Générale des Affaires Estudiantines) by **~8 December**, with the registry
stamp as proof of timeliness. Applying to autumn-2027 enrolment therefore means a call around
**19–24 October 2027, closing ~30 November / ~8 December 2027**. **`2027 dates NOT PUBLISHED — the 2020/21 call
(19 Oct → 30 Nov → 8 Dec) is given as the indicative pattern.`**

**Automatic disqualifiers to avoid:** any incomplete file is cancelled automatically; any file arriving after
the deadline is cancelled without examination; any file not registered on `best.rnu.tn` is cancelled without
examination.

**Who applies:** the **student**, in person, both online and on paper. No university involvement.

**Verdict: REALISTIC — and the single most winnable Tunisian instrument for him — but ONLY as year-1
reimbursement and year-2 support, never as the money that gets him there.** Nothing in the published conditions
excludes him: no age bar, no field bar, no grade floor, no requirement to be enrolled in Tunisia. The
competition is real and his 12–13/20 hurts on criterion 1, but his social situation helps on criterion 4, and
unlike the *bourse* there is no hard gate he fails.

- **URL:** https://best.rnu.tn/prets-universitaires/ · amounts PDF:
  https://best.rnu.tn/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/prets-montants-19-20.pdf · income-ceiling PDF linked from the
  same page · checked **2026-08-17**

### 3.2 — CNSS / CNRPS *Prêt universitaire* (social-security funds) — **SECOND STATE LOAN, DIFFERENT DOOR**

- **Awarding body:** Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale / Caisse Nationale de Retraite et de Prévoyance
  Sociale, under **décret n° 99-1544 du 15 juillet 1999**, amended by décret gouvernemental n° 2017-369 du
  15 mars 2017.
- **Eligibility:** child of a parent **affiliated to CNSS or CNRPS**, with **at least two semesters of actual
  affiliation seniority** at the date of application; the student **must not** hold a national scholarship, a
  cooperation scholarship **or a state university loan**; must not be in paid employment; **cumulative parental
  annual income ≤ 4× the annual SMIG**.
- **Amount:** equal to the annual national scholarship amount.
- **Interest: 5% per year** — explicitly stated, unlike the MESRS loan.
- **Duration:** for the duration of studies, extendable by one year in case of repetition.
- **Study abroad:** `NOT VERIFIED — the published conditions do not address foreign enrolment. Ask the CNSS
  regional office in Sousse directly.`
- **Verdict: STRETCH, and mutually exclusive with §3.1.** The killer clause is that it cannot be combined with
  a state university loan — so this is a *fallback if the MESRS loan is refused*, not an addition to it. Also
  requires a formally affiliated parent.

### 3.3 — **BCT circulaire n° 10-2025 du 5 septembre 2025 — exchange-control ceilings** (enabling, not funding)

Not a funding instrument, but it decides whether self-funding is legally executable, so it belongs here.

- **Allocation d'installation: 6,000 TND** per academic year (raised from 4,000).
- **Allocation mensuelle de séjour: 4,000 TND per month** (raised from 3,000) — the academic year is defined as
  **ten months, September to June**, capped by the duration on the enrolment certificate.
- **Tuition fees:** transferred **separately and directly to the institution against invoices** — they do
  **not** count against the living allowance.
- **Scholarship holders** may transfer only the **difference** between the circular's allowance and their
  actual scholarship. Relevant if he stacks a Stipendium/ER-GO stipend on top.
- **Academic internships abroad are now eligible** without special authorisation.
- Transfers may be made by wire, cheque, or **nominative international payment card**; a provisional file is
  accepted pending the enrolment certificate, regularised within three months.
- **Practical consequence: Tunisian exchange control is NOT the binding constraint.** 4,000 TND/month is
  ~€1,180/month, above the cost of living in every city in scope. The binding constraint is the family's actual
  dinars, not the right to move them.
- **URLs:** https://lapresse.tn/2025/09/06/etudes-a-letranger-la-banque-centrale-augmente-les-allocations-en-devises/
  · https://chaexpert.com/bct-frais-etudes-etranger-2025/ · checked **2026-08-17**

## 4. THE OTHER GENUINE OPTIONS, SORTED BY DEADLINE (earliest first)

### 4.1 — **Tunisian bank study credit — ALL savings-plan-linked** — *no deadline, but a 3-year lead time* (Task 2)

Every Tunisian bank sells the same architecture: a **Plan Épargne Études** opened years in advance, then a loan
of 2–3× the accumulated savings. There is **no Tunisian bank product that lends against admission alone**.

| Bank | Product | Terms located |
|---|---|---|
| **BIAT** | **CREDINAJAH** (on **WLEDNA** savings) | Up to **3× the savings, max 15,000 TND**. Requires a WLEDNA plan **at least 3 years old**, minimum 1,000 TND saved. Student must be **in 3rd year of higher education or above**. Explicitly covers **studies in Tunisia or abroad**. Disbursed as instalments across the study years. Repayment **up to 6 years including max 2 years' grace**. Rate **indexed to the TMM** (money-market rate; TMM tracks the BCT policy rate, **7.00%** since Jan 2026 — so expect roughly TMM + margin). |
| **STB** | **Prêt Épargne Études** (on Plan Épargne Études) | **Max 20,000 TND**. Requires accumulated capital **> 1,000 TND**, with **two-thirds of the final savings deposited at least one year** before drawdown, and the **first two university years financed from the plan's own monthly stipends**. Loan 1–4 years by field; repayment **up to 6 years with 1 year grace** after final disbursement. **Co-signer required.** Rate `NOT VERIFIED`. |
| **Amen Bank** | **Plan Épargne Campus** | Credit up to **2× accumulated savings**, sized to study duration and repayment capacity. |
| **Banque de Tunisie** | **Plan Épargne Études Avenir** | Credit up to **2× the savings, at preferential rates**, paid out as a *bourse*. |
| **Attijari Bank** | **Crédit Études** + *Stud'In* pack + "Scholarship" card | Marketed for **Tunisia and abroad**; the bank also runs the tuition-transfer service. **Amounts, rate, duration, guarantees are not published** — `NOT VERIFIED — 71 111 300 or a branch.` |
| **BH Bank** | *Prêt universitaire* | Page returned 503 repeatedly. `NOT VERIFIED — check directly.` |
| **BNA, UIB** | — | **No dedicated study-abroad credit product found.** |

### 4.2 — **AUF (Agence universitaire de la Francophonie)** — **14 September 2026 / ~9 June 2027** — *the answer is largely negative* (Task 4, priority item)

**The brief expected this to be a strong prospect. It is not, and the reason is structural.**

The AUF's student-facing instruments were enumerated from the AUF's own calls platform
(`appels-propositions.auf.org`) on **2026-08-17**. The complete list of open calls is: FOAD distance degrees;
Eugen Ionescu (**doctoral and post-doctoral only**); *Ma thèse en 180 secondes* (a competition); IFIC teacher
training; institutional/governance calls; consultant recruitments. **There is no AUF call under which an
individual student can obtain a scholarship for physical mobility to a master's programme in Europe.** AUF
student mobility, where it exists, moves through **projects held by member institutions**, is
predominantly **South–South**, and is nominated by the home university, not applied for by the student.

**What AUF does offer him:**

- **AUF / IFIC — *Formations diplômantes internationales à distance* (FOAD)**
  - **Awarding body:** AUF, with the Institut de la Formation à Distance de la Francophonie (IFIC).
  - **Tunisian eligible?** Yes — francophone students; Tunisia is an OIF member and Tunisian institutions are
    AUF members. **`Specific 2027/28 country list NOT VERIFIED.`**
  - **What it is:** **150+ degree programmes, licence to doctorate, from 45+ AUF member institutions in
    13 countries**, taken **without leaving your country of residence**.
  - **Coverage:** an *allocation de mobilité virtuelle internationale* = **partial coverage of registration
    fees** for the most deserving or least resourced, plus supervision and free access to AUF premises
    worldwide. **Not a stipend. Not full tuition.**
  - **Deadlines (2026/27 cycle, as published):** **9 June 2026** for programmes starting Sept/Oct 2026;
    **14 September 2026** for programmes starting Jan/Feb 2027. The 2027/28 call should open ~April 2027 with
    an early-June 2027 deadline. **`2027/28 dates NOT PUBLISHED — 2026/27 given as the pattern.`**
  - **Who applies:** the student, at `appels-propositions.auf.org`.
  - **⚠️ FLAG — ONLINE/DISTANCE.** Per the brief's rules this is reported separately: **no student visa, no
    post-study work rights**. It defeats the migration purpose of the plan.
  - **Verdict: REALISTIC to obtain, but it is a *different plan*, not funding for this one.** Its only
    legitimate uses here are (a) a fallback if 2027 admission fails entirely, or (b) a francophone credential
    earned from Sousse while he works. Do not put it on the main shortlist.
  - **URL:** https://appels-propositions.auf.org/appel-a-candidatures-2026-27-formations-diplomantes-internationales-a-distance
    · catalogue https://l.auf.org/FOAD-FD · checked **2026-08-17**

- **AUF Eugen Ionescu (Romania) — NOT ELIGIBLE.** Doctoral students and researcher-teachers only. He holds a
  licence. https://www.auf.org/projet/bourses-de-recherche-doctorale-et-post-doctorale-eugen-ionescu/

### 4.3 — ⭐ **Billboard France × Amazon Music — *Bourse d'études*** — **~15 July 2027** — **PRIORITY FIND, directions 3 + 4** (Task 5)

- **Awarding body:** Billboard France, with the support of **Amazon Music**.
- **Tunisian eligible?** **`NOT VERIFIED — no nationality condition is published either way.`** The published
  conditions are about the *programme* and the *project*, not the passport. **This is the single most important
  thing to email about**: `bourse@fr.billboard.com`.
- **Eligibility as published:** admitted to a **master's year (M1 or M2)** **in a French institution**; the
  funded year **must not be under an apprenticeship contract**; must demonstrate a **professional project in
  the music industry**; **extra-curricular music projects are valued in selection**; **financial need is a key
  assessment factor**.
- **Coverage:** ***financement intégral d'une année d'études*** — full funding of one year of study — plus
  **10 meetings with music-industry professionals, 3 Billboard France training sessions, and personalised
  support in finding an internship or work-study placement**.
- **Number:** **2 awards per year.**
- **Deadline:** 2026 edition ran **18 June → 15 July 2026, midnight French time**. For a 2027-28 master's year,
  expect **~18 June → ~15 July 2027**. **`2027 dates NOT PUBLISHED.`**
- **Who applies:** the **student**, by email, with CV, motivation letter and supporting materials — **after
  securing admission**, which fits the French calendar well (admission by spring, bourse in July).
- **Language:** Tier A — French.
- **Verdict: STRETCH, but the best-matched competitive award found for this student anywhere in this scope.**
  Two awards a year is brutal odds. But every soft criterion favours him: an authentic music-industry
  motivation, genuine financial need, and extra-curricular music work — and **grades are not a published
  criterion**, which is precisely where he normally loses. It also stacks with France, an OPEN country. **He
