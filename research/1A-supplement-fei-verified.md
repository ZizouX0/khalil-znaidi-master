## Why the timing matters more than the cost

A 4–6 month turnaround is a **hard scheduling constraint**, not a detail. For an
autumn 2027 intake, a statement of comparability needed at application time must
be requested by roughly **late 2026 / very early 2027**. If the student decides
late that he needs one, it cannot be rushed.

Two things follow for the action plan:

1. Do not order this speculatively for every country — it covers **one
   qualification per file**, and most routes will not require it (see 1A: the
   licence is a 180-credit award by Tunisian law; the free *supplément au
   diplôme* plus an *attestation de réussite* from FSEG Sousse resolves the
   146 question in most cases).
2. Order it early **only** if a specific target programme demands a formal
   equivalency statement. Identify whether any shortlisted programme does, and
   put the request date on the timeline working backwards from its deadline.
