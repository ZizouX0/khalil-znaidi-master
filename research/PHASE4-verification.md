# PHASE 4 — INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

**All checks performed 2026-08-17.** Every figure below was re-checked against the
official university page or government/ministry source. Aggregators were used only
to *locate* documents, never to confirm a number.

## Bottom line

Four of the shortlist items survive intact. **One collapses entirely**: the Tunisian
MESRS *bourse universitaire à l'étranger* is a 10-place, STEM-only, age-capped
competition for currently-enrolled final-year students — the student is ineligible on
at least five independent counts, and the published minimum is **12.5/20, not 12/20**.
Two further corrections: the FH Kufstein MOI letter must demonstrate **English** at B2
(so a letter from a French/Arabic-medium faculty does not work), and the NAWA Banach
stipend is **PLN 2,500**, not PLN 1,800. Several deadlines in the earlier files are from
the 2026/27 cycle and have already passed; 2027/28 dates are almost all unpublished.

## Corrections in detail — the high-value lines

### 4. Tunisian MESRS bourse — the claim does not survive contact with the circular

Primary source: **Circulaire no. 61/25 of the Ministre de l'Enseignement supérieur et
de la Recherche scientifique, dated 23 October 2025**, "منح لإعداد دراسات الماجستير
والدكتوراه بالخارج بعنوان السنة الجامعية 2026-2027" (13 pp., scanned Arabic), at
`https://best.rnu.tn/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Circulaire-61-25-Bourses-Mastere-et-Doctorat-2026-2027.pdf`
(index page: `https://best.rnu.tn/offre-nationale-des-bourses-pour-des-etudes-de-mastere-et-de-doctorat-a-letranger-au-titre-de-lannee-universitaire-2026-2027/`).

**Announcement no. 1 (EU) offers 10 grants in total**, split across three fields only:
التحول الطاقي (energy transition) 2 · الصناعة الذكية 4.0 (smart industry) 4 ·
الذكاء الاصطناعي (AI) 4. Eligible *licence* mentions are physics/energy, industrial,
electrical, mechanical, electronics, computer science and ICT. **There is no marketing,
business or management mention anywhere in the table.** Announcement no. 2 (Canada) is
likewise STEM-only (AI, data science, quantum computing, cybersecurity, MEMS).

Published conditions (الشروط المبدئية للترشح), quoted:
- «معدل سنوات الدراسة (م.س.د): ≥ 12.5/20» — **12.5/20, not 12/20.**
- «مسار منتظم مع عدم الرسوب أو الانقطاع خلال كامل سنوات الدراسة الجامعية» — an unbroken
  path with **no failed or repeated year** from the bac to the application date.
- «عمر المترشح: لا يفوق 24 سنة في غرة سبتمبر 2026 للمتحصلين على شهادة إجازة» — **age ≤ 24
  on 1 September** for licence holders.

Eligible applicants: «الطلبة المرشّحون من طرف مؤسّساتهم والمسجّلون **بالسنة الثالثة من
شهادة إجازة** بعنوان السنة الجامعية 2025-2026» — students *nominated by their institution*
and **currently enrolled in the third year of the licence**. Graduates cannot apply.

Registration abroad must be «بالسنة الأولى ماجستير **بحث**» — first year of a **research**
master's. A taught/professional master does not qualify.

**Verdict for this student: BLOCKING on five independent counts** — field (marketing not
listed), GPA (12–13/20 vs 12.5/20 minimum, and he is competing against nominated
top-ranked students), status (already graduated, not a current 3rd-year), age cap, and
programme type (research master's only). Filing route confirmed: institution nominates →
student registers on best.rnu.tn → physical file to the **university presidency's bureau
d'ordre**; the 2026/27 file deadline was 19/12/2025.

**Amount, separately:** best.rnu.tn still displays "800 € — 600 € bourse + 200 € prêt" for
the EU. That page is out of date. An **arrêté of 24 July 2026, published in the JORT that
day**, raises the *bourse spécifique* for EU countries to **€1,000/month = €800 grant +
€200 loan**, with the *bourse d'alternance* also set at €1,000/month. Reported by
Mosaïque FM, Le Temps, Directinfo, African Manager and L'Économiste Maghrébin, all
citing the JORT text. Use €1,000, not €800 — but note the eligibility bar above makes
the amount academic for this student.

### 1d. FH Kufstein medium-of-instruction letter — accepted, but not the way it helps him

Primary source: **"English language skills and certificates", FH Kufstein Tirol, October
2025**, `https://www.fh-kufstein.ac.at/pdf-website/studium/ueber-das-studium/accepted-language-certificate-admission.pdf`.
Verbatim:

> "As an alternative to the language certificates listed above, applicants can submit a
> certificate or an official letter (MOI) from a university/school that **clearly
> demonstrates the language competence at CEFR level B2 or higher**."

The MOI route is real and explicitly offered — that part of the earlier claim stands. But
the letter must **demonstrate English competence at B2**, not merely state a language of
instruction. A letter from FSEG Sousse attesting French/Arabic-medium study proves the
opposite of what is required. Exemption without proof is limited to citizens of the USA,
Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. Duolingo, Babbel and OLS are explicitly
rejected. Proof may be supplied after application but must arrive **by the start of the
programme**. **Practical conclusion: he needs IELTS Academic 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 72 / PTE 60
/ Cambridge / LanguageCert. Budget for the test.**

### 6d. NAWA Banach stipend

The 2026 call (`https://nawa.gov.pl/en/nawa/news/call-for-applications-banach-nawa-2026`)
states **"monthly scholarship of PLN 2,500"** plus a **PLN 2,500 travel allowance**,
exemption from tuition at public universities, an NAWA-funded preparatory year on the
Polish track, and a Polish language and culture course on the English track. The PLN 1,800
figure in circulation is superseded. Results by 31 July 2026; the call closes early if the
per-country-group application limit is hit, so applying on day one matters.

### 1b. ÖH fee

€26.20 per semester plus €0.70 insurance = **€26.90** for winter semester 2026/27, set by
statutory formula and announced by 1 May each year. So FH Kufstein all-in ≈ **€726.72 +
€53.80 = €780.52/year**.

## Confirmations worth quoting

**FH Kufstein tuition** — `https://www.fh-kufstein.ac.at/en/admissions/tuition-fees`:
third-country nationals pay **"EUR 363.36 per semester"**, with a **"EUR 726.72"** deposit
covering the first year payable with the online application, refundable (less expenses and
FX differences) if the applicant is not admitted, misses the interview, or declines.

**FH Kufstein Digital Marketing entry** — "Information on Admission, Digital Marketing
(FH Kufstein Tirol), full-time", version dated 08.10.2025,
`https://www.fh-kufstein.ac.at/pdf-website/studium/studiengaenge/dim/zugangsvoraussetzungen-dim-vz-en.pdf`:

> "Subjects relevant to this master's program are bachelor's degree programs or equivalent
> post-secondary education degrees in social and economic sciences … that cover the core
> subject areas of Marketing, Communication, Management and Business administration …
> **summarily treated in a total of at least 30 ECTS**."

No 180-ECTS clause appears anywhere in the document. It also allows: "Applicants who have
completed a degree in a different field … but has professional experience in a relevant
field may also be considered." **146-ECTS verdict: LIKELY OK.** Places are limited and an
admission procedure applies under §11 FHG, so meeting the requirement "does not constitute
a claim to a study place."

**FH Kufstein Data Science** — `https://www.fh-kufstein.ac.at/en/master/data-science-intelligent-analytics-ft`:
"Prior knowledge of mathematics and statistics (8 ECTS) and computer science (6 ECTS) is
required for admission." and "Applicants who lack this knowledge can make up for it with
our **free video-based preparatory course**." MSc, 120 ECTS, 100% English. Confirmed.

**FH Kufstein deadlines** — `https://www.fh-kufstein.ac.at/en/admissions/admission-deadlines`:
"For applicants from third countries, the application deadline is **03.03.2026**", not
applying to holders of a valid Schengen residence permit. This is the 2026/27 date and has
passed. Expect ~03.03.2027 for autumn 2027; **not yet published — re-check from autumn 2026.**

**GIOCA** — `https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/gioca` and `.../how-to-enrol`: Second Cycle
Degree (Laurea Magistrale), class **LM-76 R**, 2 years, Bologna, delivered entirely in
English. Entry: "a **first level degree (Bachelor's)** or an equivalent degree in any
discipline from a foreign university that has been deemed eligible, **the length of the
degree must be of at least three years**." — a years-based test, so **146-ECTS verdict:
LIKELY OK**. "Admission to the degree programme is limited to **50 places**" with
"**25 places** for non-EU Citizens with Residence Abroad." English: "Candidates must
possess a minimum **B2 level or equivalent**."

**Unibo fees** — `https://www.unibo.it/en/study/enrolment-fees-and-other-procedures/degree-programmes/tuition-fees-and-exemptions`:
full exemption from the variable part where "the indicator of the family unit's financial
situation is equal to or lower than **€27,000**", leaving only **€157.04** (stamp duty €16
+ ER.GO regional tax €140 + insurance €1.04). **Caveat not to lose:** a non-EU applicant
with income abroad must obtain an *ISEE parificato*, or else fall back to the separate
"reduced fixed fee for citizens of particularly poverty-stricken and developing countries"
route, whose amount is on a different page. Verify which route applies before budgeting €157.

**UMA prices** — Anexo C, "Curso Académico 2026/2027",
`https://www.uma.es/seccion-de-estudiantes/navegador_de_ficheros/Guia_de_Master_2026-27/descargar/Anexo_C_PreciosPublicos/ANEXO_C.pdf`:

> "El precio de las primeras matrículas de los másteres habilitantes queda fijado en 12,62
> euros y el de los **no habilitantes en 13,68 €**."

and, verbatim, the foreign-student clause:

> "…Los estudiantes afectados deberán presentar en la Secretaría de su respectivo Centro,
> **con anterioridad al día 1 de noviembre de 2026**, el documento acreditativo de haber
> obtenido (o resguardo de haberlo solicitado) la mencionada condición de residente, o
> autorización de estancia por estudios. Caso contrario se procederá a efectuar una
> liquidación complementaria que **podrá alcanzar el 100% del coste real** de la prestación
> de los citados servicios."

The rate is unchanged from the 2023/24 annex the earlier agent found — that annex was old
but its number was right. Programme is 90 ECTS over 1.5 years, RUCT 4315121, *máster
universitario oficial*, not habilitante. Total ≈ **€1,296**. Andalusian framework:
Decreto 98/2023 as modified by Decreto 142/2025 (BOJA 2025/172).
UMA 2026/27 enrolment windows: Fase 1 (foreign students) 3–24 July 2026; Fases 2–3 to
15 October 2026 — 2027/28 not yet published.

**Stipendium Hungaricum** — official BA/MA/OTM Call for Applications 2026/27,
`https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BA_MA_OTM_Call_for_Applications_2026_27.pdf`:
"**HUF 43,700 (cca EUR 110)** contribution to the living expenses in Hungary for 12
months"; deadline "**15 January 2026, 2 p.m. (Central European Time)**"; Tunisian Republic
listed as a Sending Partner for full-degree programmes. The 250-place Tunisian quota is
published on the Tunisian side, not the Hungarian: MESRS notice `https://www.mes.tn/image.php?id=19187`,
reported as 250 for 2026/27 — 35 bachelor's, 15 one-tier master's, **130 master's**, 70
doctoral — under the MoU between MESRS and the Hungarian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and
Trade. Note the MoU is described as covering **2023–2025**; whether it has been renewed for
2027/28 is unconfirmed. Applicants must register with MESRS as well as apply online.

**UK Graduate Route** — `https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa`, verbatim: "**2 years if you
apply on or before 31 December 2026**" and "**18 months if you apply on or after 1 January
2027**"; "If you have a PhD or other doctoral qualification, it will last for 3 years."
**Confirmed exactly as claimed.** Practical note: a student starting autumn 2027 graduates
in 2028–29 and will therefore get **18 months**, not 2 years. Any UK-vs-Ireland comparison
must use 18 months.

**France 1.5 × SMIC** — primary source now found. **Arrêté du 22 mai 2026 relatif au
relèvement du salaire minimum de croissance**, `https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000054126589`:
gross hourly SMIC **€12.31** from **1 June 2026**, i.e. **€1,867.02/month** at 35 h.
1.5 × = **€2,800.53/month = €33,606.36/year gross**. The claimed €33,606 is right.

*Caveat, stated plainly:* the legal hook is **CESEDA Article L.421-4**
(`https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042776787`), which says the
student with a master's-level French diploma and a contract "en relation avec sa
formation" gets the permit where remuneration exceeds "**un seuil déterminé par décret**"
— and permits under L.421-1/L.421-3 are then issued "sans que lui soit opposable la
situation de l'emploi". I could **not** locate the implementing article that states the
1.5 multiplier: the regulatory sub-section (R421-1 to D421-6) contains only the diploma
list (D421-6), and the "1,5 fois" figures elsewhere in CESEDA belong to the EU Blue Card
(R421-21 A), which is a different scheme. The 1.5 multiplier **is** stated on a French
government site — Préfecture de Loire-Atlantique,
`https://www.loire-atlantique.gouv.fr/Demarches/Demarches-etrangers/Travail-des-etrangers`:
"Le salarié est titulaire d'un diplôme au moins équivalent au master, l'emploi est en lien
avec son diplôme et rémunéré au moins **1,5x le SMIC**". Treat the multiplier as confirmed
administrative practice on a .gouv.fr source; the underlying decree article remains
unlocated. `NOT VERIFIED — check directly` at
`https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070158/LEGISCTA000042800916/`
if the exact decree reference is ever needed.

**NAWA Banach** — Tunisia is one of the 36 Polish Aid countries listed on the Polish
government portal `https://study.gov.pl/stefan-banach-nawa-programme`. No field-of-study
restriction: "full-time second-cycle studies conducted in Polish or English at
universities supervised by the Minister of Science and Higher Education", any discipline.
The "Group 3" label could not be confirmed on nawa.gov.pl and the Group-3 list circulating
online includes Egypt, DRC, Yemen and Zambia, which do not appear in the current
36-country list — treat that grouping as **stale**. Group membership matters only because
the call closes when a group's application limit is reached.
