## 1. BOTTOM LINE

I went through the **entire official EACEA Erasmus Mundus catalogue — all 220 currently
funded programmes, 11 pages, scraped and read in full** — and the honest result is
narrow. There is **no Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in music marketing, artist
promotion, music business or music industry management. Zero. Direction 3 and
direction 4 are empty at EMJM level**, and I will not stretch a sound-design
conservatory degree or an art-market degree into being one. There is also **no EMJM in
marketing analytics or AI-for-marketing** — the catalogue has a dozen excellent data/AI
masters (BDMA, EMAI, DEAI, EDISS, CoDaS, NeuroData, MULTICOM, EMLDS) and **every one of
them requires a computer-science, engineering or mathematics first degree**, which makes
them hard blocks for a marketing licence, not stretches. Direction 2 (digital business)
yields essentially **one** genuine match, CLIDE. What the catalogue *does* offer him is a
cluster of **management / creative-industries-management / service-innovation** programmes
that accept a business or social-science bachelor from any discipline. The realistic
target list is **five programmes: SDSI, CLIDE, GOALS, MARIHE and MAGMa** — and of those,
**SDSI and CLIDE are the two where his CRM platform and Renault internship make him
genuinely competitive rather than merely eligible.** The funding is as good as advertised:
a full participation-cost waiver plus **€1,400/month for up to 24 months (max €33,600)**,
and as a Tunisian he sits inside the **NDICI / Southern Neighbourhood top-up window**,
which on some programmes means explicitly ring-fenced extra scholarships. **The earliest
live deadline is MARIHE, 21 September 2026 — thirty-five days from today** — and the main
2027 wave opens **1 October–1 December 2026** and closes **January–February 2027**.
**He has no English test, and that is now the binding constraint on the whole EMJM route.**

## 3. HOW THE MONEY ACTUALLY WORKS — verified, and one correction to the brief

Source: Erasmus+ Programme Guide, Part B, KA1 — Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters. Checked 2026-08-17.

- Scholarship = **participation-cost waiver + €1,400/month × programme duration**, i.e. **€16,800 (12 months) / €25,200 (18 months) / €33,600 (24 months)**.
- The €1,400/month is defined as covering **"travel, visa, installation and subsistence costs."** **There is no separate travel allowance on top** — the brief's "tuition + travel + ~€1,400/month" slightly overstates it. The single monthly figure is the whole non-tuition package. It is still generous: €1,400/month is above the cost of living in most host cities.
- Consortia **"cannot charge student application fees"** and **"cannot charge tuition fees or other mandatory costs"** to scholarship holders. **Scholarship holder = €0 tuition, always.**
- **Nationality cap: "no more than 10% of the total number of scholarships awarded during project implementation should be awarded to candidates from the same nationality."** On a programme awarding ~12/year over 4 cohorts (~48 total), that is roughly **5 Tunisians across the entire project life**. GOALS states this as "no more than three nationals from the same country"; CLIDE states "no more than five scholarships" per nationality. **He is competing against other Tunisians for a hard-capped slice, not against the whole pool.**
- **NDICI top-up — this genuinely favours him.** The Programme Guide provides for **additional externally-funded (NDICI) scholarships for targeted regions, up to 18 extra per project, outside the standard allocation**. Tunisia sits in the Southern Neighbourhood and qualifies. **SDSI publishes this concretely: "An additional 5 scholarships support students from NDICI and IPA III countries."** **Rule of thumb for him: prefer programmes that publish an NDICI/IPA III window. It is the single biggest odds-improver available.**
- Typical self-funded participation cost for a Partner Country student where charged: **€8,000/year** (GOALS, SDSI, CLIDE at €4,000/semester). Mundus Journalism is higher (€9,000 + €12,000 = €21,000 total). MAGMa via Glasgow is far higher (€22,224/year international). **All of these are above the brief's €15,000 ceiling in total terms, so for this student EMJMs are a scholarship-or-nothing route — there is no self-funded fallback.**

## 4. THE 146 ECTS VERDICT ACROSS EMJMs — read this before anything else

This is the most consistent risk in the whole EMJM route. **Six of the programmes I verified state a literal numeric 180 ECTS requirement in their own wording:**

| Programme | Exact wording | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| MARIHE | "hold a first university degree, with a minimum duration of three years of studies (full time), **corresponding to 180 ECTS**" | **BORDERLINE** — but note the wording leads with *three years of study*, which is exactly the framing Phase 1 said he passes. Best-worded of the six. |
| SDSI | "a bachelor's degree with **a minimum of 180 ECTS credits** or equivalent professional diploma at LQF/EQF level 6-7" | **BORDERLINE** |
| GOALS | "a Higher Education (HE) undergraduate degree (e.g., **Bachelor, Licence, 180ECTS**) or a recognised equivalent level" | **BORDERLINE, mildest of all** — it explicitly names *Licence* as a qualifying category, which is the Tunisian degree name. |
| EGEI | "**180 ECTS credits** in a University study programme … in economics, applied economics or related disciplines" | BORDERLINE on credits, **BLOCKING on subject** (see below) |
| IMATEC | "a Bachelor degree (**minimum 180 ECTS credits**) in pure or applied science" | **BLOCKING on subject** regardless |
| DIGICREA | "Bachelor's degree with **minimum 180 ECTS**… candidates with **150 ECTS** at application time may be considered **with a sworn statement guaranteeing 180 ECTS before programme start**" | **USEFUL PRECEDENT** — see below |

**Two things follow.**

1. **DIGICREA's 150-ECTS clause is the single most useful sentence I found for this student.** It proves EMJM consortia routinely handle sub-180 transcripts by accepting a **sworn statement**. That is precisely the instrument the FSEG Sousse attestation provides (6 semesters / 3 years / 180 credits / confers access to Mastère). **He should attach that attestation, unprompted, to every EMJM application**, and cite the *supplément au diplôme*.
2. **EMMIE is a hard BLOCK and should be struck off.** HEC Liège states: *"Candidates must have completed at minimum an undergraduate degree with a **minimum of 240 ECTS**."* That is a four-year requirement, not a transcript artefact. No attestation fixes it. Do not apply.

## 7. HONEST ODDS — the part he needs to hear

**Erasmus Mundus is a genuinely long-odds route, and the brief is right that it is still worth applying to.** Both things are true. A funded EMJM place is worth roughly **€50,000–€70,000** to him (tuition waiver plus €33,600 stipend plus the visa and the post-study work right). At those stakes a 5% chance is worth three applications; at 2% it is still worth one. **But it must not be his only plan.** Applications 3A should sit alongside, not instead of, the France/Spain/Italy/Poland public-university work.

**Where he is genuinely competitive:**
1. **SDSI** — business background accepted, no GPA floor, IELTS 5.5, **5 NDICI-reserved scholarships**, and his CRM project *is* the discipline. **His best single shot.**
2. **CLIDE** — brand-new programme, ~half the cohort funded, five scholarships per nationality, B2 English, Moroccan partner, latest deadline. **His best shot on timing.**
3. **GOALS** — *Licence* named in the entry wording, no GPA floor, IELTS 6.0, May deadline. Sector fit is the question, not eligibility.
4. **MARIHE** — any discipline, no GPA floor, tuition-free for everyone. **For 2028, not 2027.**
5. **MAGMa** — best subject match for direction 4, but conditional on Glasgow confirming his grade. **Email first, apply second.**

**Where he is not competitive, and should not spend effort:** every data/AI EMJM (BDMA, EMAI, DEAI, EDISS, CoDaS, NeuroData, EMLDS, MULTICOM), EGEI, IMATEC, EMMIE, Mundus Journalism, and all the portfolio-gated arts programmes (RESOUND, RE_PLAY, Theatre SPACEs, Kino Eyes, DocNomads, FilmMemory, Re:Anima).

**The binding constraint is English, not the GPA and not the 146 ECTS.** Every programme on the target list requires a certificate at application. **He should book IELTS Academic now, targeting 6.5** — that single score unlocks SDSI (needs 5.5), CLIDE (B2), GOALS (6.0–6.5) and MARIHE (6.5) all at once, and there is no substitute for it.
