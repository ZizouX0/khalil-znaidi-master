# CONTRADICTION RESOLVED — a plain RNCP niveau 7 title DOES grant the French post-study permit

**Agents in conflict:** Agent 2A (France) held ICART, IESA and ESG Nantes **off the
shortlist** on the grounds that plain *titre RNCP niveau 7* qualifications "carry a
real post-study-work risk" because the APS list covers only *master / MS /
CGE-labelled MSc*. Agent 3H then traced the actual statutory chain.

**Verdict: 2A was wrong. 3H is right, and the legal chain is documented.**

## The chain, as established

1. **CESEDA L.422-10** admits diplomas *"figurant sur une liste fixée par décret"*.
2. That list is the **Arrêté du 12 mai 2011**, whose Art. 1, 2° reads
   *"les titres et diplômes inscrits au **niveau I** au RNCP"* — consolidated
   version still in force as checked 2026-08-17.
3. **Décret n° 2019-14 of 8 January 2019** reclassified every *niveau I*
   certification to **niveau 7 or 8**.
4. Therefore **niveau 7 sits on the statutory list.**

`service-public.fr` F17319 corroborates, listing even a *licence professionnelle*
as qualifying.

**Consequence:** ICART, IESA and ESG Nantes move from *"real risk"* to
**"low residual risk"**. The only remaining exposure is L.422-10's separate
requirement that the institution be *"habilité au plan national"* — worth a
written confirmation from the school, but **not a reason to keep a programme off
the shortlist**.

## A second, unplanned resolution — the number 3I could not verify

Agent 3I called the French student→employee salary threshold *"the most decisive
single number"* in its whole analysis, and had to mark it `NOT VERIFIED` after
Légifrance and Campus France both 404'd. It estimated ≈€33,606/yr from
1.5 × SMIC.

3H independently reports the RECE conversion threshold as **">€2,800.53 gross
per month."**

**€2,800.53 × 12 = €33,606.36.** That matches 3I's figure to the cent.

Two agents reaching the same number by different routes is strong corroboration.
**Treat ≈€33,600/yr as CONFIRMED** rather than estimated. 3I's central argument —
that a junior CRM/data role in Paris clears this line and a junior music or
promotions role does not — therefore stands on a verified threshold.

Also verified: **RECE is 12 months, non-renewable**. And **Passeport Talent does
not rescue a low salary** — it requires 2× SMIC (≈€44,000). A reported €35,000
young-graduate floor is `NOT VERIFIED`.

## The benchmark finding that reframes the private-school question

3H's most useful structural point: **EM Strasbourg** is part of Université de
Strasbourg, holds **AACSB + EQUIS**, and awards a *diplôme national de master* —
at **€254 + CVEC**, or ≈€4,055 if the differentiated rate applies.

So the bar every private school must clear is: *beat a triple-accredited public
grande école costing €4,055/year.* Most do not come close. This should be stated
plainly in the deliverables — it is the cleanest way to explain why the private
sector is mostly poor value for this student.
